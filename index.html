When on, a trip starts by itself once you drive faster than about 14 km/h and ends after 3 minutes stopped. The app has to stay open.

Storage

You're not signed in. Trips are saved on this phone only (in this browser).

iCloud sign-in isn't available: it needs an Apple Developer account and server setup. Instead, export a backup file and save it to iCloud Drive from the share sheet, then import it on any device.

Habits tracked: speeding and severe speeding (20 over your alert speed), hard braking, hard acceleration, sharp turns, swerving, possible impacts, long drives without a break, night and rush-hour driving, smooth driving, touching the screen while moving, and leaving the app while moving. Screen touches can come from a passenger and leaving the app includes maps or music, so treat these as a guide. Real road speed limits aren't available, so set the alert to suit you.

Open automatically when you drive

Download the shortcut, then use it in an automation so this app opens when Driving mode starts or your car's Bluetooth connects.

Download shortcut
  1. Tap Download shortcut, open the file, and tap Add Shortcut.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, then Automation, then +.
  3. Choose Bluetooth (pick your car) or Focus, then Driving.
  4. Add Action, Run Shortcut, and choose "Open trip tracker url".
  5. Tap Next, choose Run Immediately, then Done.

If your phone won't open the file, make it by hand: Shortcuts, +, Open URLs, and paste this page's address.

Add to Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with its own icon.

Good to know: the Home Screen app keeps its own saved trips, separate from Safari. Your Driving shortcut opens Safari, so pick one, or move trips between them with Export and Import backup.
